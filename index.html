The Story

Nobody leaves
Vice City clean

The neon never went out. It just got smarter. In 2045 the waterfront is a corporate arcology, the swamp is a data farm, and every debt in Leonida is denominated in something you can't hold.

Rico Márquez spent nine years running cars between the port and the towers. One delivery goes missing. One manifest doesn't match. Now he's holding a debt to a bank that doesn't have a building, and the only people who'll touch him are the ones who set him up.

Across a city that rewrites its own skyline every quarter, three people chase the same ledger — through the arcades, the yacht basin, the dead malls and the flood zones the developers forgot to buy.

It ends where it always ends. On the water, at dawn.
